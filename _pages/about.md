My background combines a robotics MSc and quadruped motion-control work with four years of industrial servo motion systems.

I am currently an Application Engineer at [Siemens](https://www.siemens.com/cn/zh.html) in Beijing, working on motion control — SINAMICS S120/S200/V90 drive systems, reusable SCL axis-control libraries covering homing, cam profiles and diagnostics, and the engineering tooling that generates and validates a machine's control project instead of hand-building it. During my 2022 internship at [Geely Research Institute](https://www.geely.com/), I worked on YoboGo quadruped motion control with an MIT Mini Cheetah-derived C++ stack on an Up Board. Colleagues handled Jetson-side SLAM and LiDAR.

I am moving back towards robotics, which is where the MSc and the quadruped work started and where the motion control work has been pointing all along. Industrial servo control and robot motion control are the same discipline in different vocabulary: trajectory generation, closed-loop position control, coordinated multi-axis motion, and the unglamorous business of what the system does when a sensor lies or an axis faults mid-move.
